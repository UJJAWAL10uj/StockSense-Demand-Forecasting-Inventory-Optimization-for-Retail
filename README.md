# StockSense: Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization for Retail

## Overview

StockSense is a retail demand forecasting and inventory optimization project focused on predicting weekly demand at the SKU level and using those forecasts to support better inventory replenishment decisions.

The project will combine demand forecasting, inventory analysis, safety stock calculations, and reorder point recommendations to help retailers maintain appropriate inventory levels while reducing stockouts and excess inventory.

## Dataset Description

The project will use a retail sales dataset containing historical transaction information such as:

* Date or transaction date
* Product or SKU
* Store or sales location
* Quantity sold
* Sales or revenue-related information, where available

The dataset will be explored and cleaned before being used for demand forecasting and inventory analysis.

## Problem Statement

### Objective

Predict weekly demand for each Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) using historical retail sales data.

### Goal

The primary goal is to support inventory decisions by reducing:

* **Stockouts:** Situations where products are unavailable when customers want to purchase them.
* **Overstock:** Situations where excessive inventory is held beyond expected demand.

### Action

Use demand forecasts to recommend appropriate reorder points for individual products. The analysis will consider historical demand patterns and inventory-related factors to determine when replenishment should be initiated.

### Expected Impact

An effective demand forecasting and replenishment strategy can help retailers:

* Reduce stockout-related lost sales
* Reduce excess inventory and associated holding costs
* Improve inventory availability
* Support data-driven replenishment decisions
* Improve overall inventory management efficiency

### Week 5 — Inventory Optimization

* Combine demand forecasts with inventory calculations.
